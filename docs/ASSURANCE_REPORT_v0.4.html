What the results do and do not establish

All seven authored geometric/temporal unsafe episodes were detected by reconstructed terminal safety checks. The three additional rejections by process verification concern unsupported recovery claims, missing action proposals, and event reordering. This does not demonstrate incremental physical unsafe-action detection beyond a strong safety-aware baseline.

A coherently forged but self-consistent sensor/pose record still passes the standalone monitor. Hash continuity and separate code paths do not establish source authenticity or the physical truth of observations.
