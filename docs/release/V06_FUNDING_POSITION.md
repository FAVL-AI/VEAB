# Funder-facing VEAB status — scope-controlled (12 November 2026)

VEAB is an embodied-agent evaluation research project with a read-only assurance analytics product track. The working software includes local replay and agent-independent verification controls for bounded traces and synthetic fixtures. The v0.6 engineering candidate adds a strict shared input-tape contract for native simulation studies, but does not yet provide a native Isaac Lab or MuJoCo evidence bundle from this runtime.

**Research question:** Do contract-conditioned process checks identify additional independently adjudicated failures beyond a reproduced, safety-aware terminal grader at an acceptable false rejection rate?

**Project-stage claim:** research prototype; no funder-defined TRL rating has been independently assessed. A TRL 5–6 deployment claim is pending operational evidence, an eligible deployment partner and the funding programme's specific eligibility assessment.

**Prospective work:** freeze scenarios and independent scene clusters, measure sensitivity/specificity and assessment overhead, run calibrated native cross-simulator experiments, and conduct a read-only shadow-mode customer workflow study subject to agreements and safety/data protection assessment.

**Commercial product boundary:** VEAB Replay analyses recorded robotics evidence. It does not control robots, replace functional safety engineering, or confer external certification. Cost and schedule figures are estimates until itemised and independently reviewed.

**No funding award, committed deployer, native simulator validation or scientific superiority is claimed in this note.**
