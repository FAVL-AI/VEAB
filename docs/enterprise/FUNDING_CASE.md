# VEAB Assurance Studio — grant and customer funding concept

## Positioning

A model- and harness-agnostic embodied-AI evidence and assurance platform, designed to expose unsafe or unsupported task completion under controlled disturbances. The open scientific protocol builds credibility; the commercial studio offers customer isolation, managed evaluation, auditable records, human review and operational reporting.

**Initial buyer:** robotics validation lead, industrial safety/assurance engineer, or autonomous-system engineering manager. **Use case:** read-only review of recorded and shadow-mode warehouse/hospital-adjacent mobile robot navigation. This is *not* a production robot safety controller.

## Specific need

Operational buyers need to distinguish reaching a goal from completing a task within permitted trajectories and verified constraints, and to know how model choice, safety gate, harness, environment and faults affect the result. Competitors already offer robot testing, agent evaluations and formal methods. VEAB's intended differentiation is a reproducible, scenario-clustered joint assurance contract spanning those dimensions; comparative novelty remains to be proven.

## Package into funded work packages

**WP1 (Months 1–3, scientific)** freeze protocols, independent validators, negative controls, sample-size analysis, publication-grade comparisons.

**WP2 (Months 3–6, engine calibration)** native Isaac Lab and MuJoCo experiments with matched geometry, contacts, forces and timing; separate Gazebo/ROS 2 middleware compliance study.

**WP3 (Months 4–9, enterprise platform)** external identity management, secure evidence custody, isolated execution, tenant security, backups, observability.

**WP4 (Months 7–12, industrial pilot)** supervised shadow-mode robot telemetry, separately instrumented reference monitors, blinded evaluation, operator usability and customer acceptance.

## Funding eligibility is conditional

Seek an eligible UK SME lead, an operational industrial deployer, and an independent academic/safety evaluation partner. Verify grant scope, readiness level, entity eligibility, co-funding, overheads, IP and state subsidy rules **against current official calls** before applying. Existing research-code tests do not prove TRL 5–6. Do not promise a certified safety result or patentable novelty absent an IP and prior-art assessment.

## Go/no-go evidence

- Scientifically: >=10 percentage-point incremental sensitivity on the prespecified error class vs a strong safety-aware comparator, <=5% false rejection, scene-level CIs, blinded external labels.
- Engineering: native engine traces, provenance-constrained telemetry, no cross-tenant access in external security testing, restore success, measured SLA baseline.
- Commercial: at least one contracted paid pilot or customer-accepted evaluation with quantified review-time reduction and defined limitations.

Record negative and adverse findings as research outputs. Underperformance versus a strong comparator should trigger method refinement rather than withheld reporting.
