# VEAB Replay — controlled website copy candidate

**VEAB Replay:** Read-only robot-run analysis for researchers and assurance engineers. Import eligible recorded robot or simulator traces, inspect task progress and safety-relevant events, and generate reproducible evidence summaries with explicitly marked data limitations.

**Research maturity:** Selected deterministic toy-world and synthetic negative-control mechanisms have been demonstrated in local development. Native Isaac Lab/MuJoCo calibration, physical measurement, organisationally independent review and production operations remain pending.

**Critical boundary:** VEAB does not issue robot control commands; its reports support human engineering judgement and do not replace a robot's independent functional safety systems or regulatory assessment.
